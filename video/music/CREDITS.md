# Music credits

The montage uses `MUSIC_PATH` when a video's `make.sh` sets it, otherwise the first audio
file of this folder (alphabetical). The track is levelled to -16 LUFS and ducked under the
voice: -28 dB while someone talks, -20 dB in the gaps.

Add one line per track, with the link to its licence:

| File                                               | Title · artist | Source     | Licence    | Credit required |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ---------- | ---------- | --------------- |
| `video/assets/background-advertising.mp3` (Button) | to fill in     | to fill in | to fill in | to fill in      |

When the licence requires a credit, copy it into `final/<component>-description.txt`.
